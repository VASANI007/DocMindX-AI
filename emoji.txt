6. TECHNICAL & EMAIL CLIENT COMPATIBILITY TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HTML Entity Format:
   Email templates me raw emojis ki jagah unke HTML Decimal Entities (e.g. &#128274;)
   use karne se older email clients (Outlook desktop, old webmail) me character corruption
   (jaise `??` ya black square) nahi hota.

2. Subject Line Usage:
   Subject lines me direct emoji daal sakte hain (e.g. "[DocMindX AI] 🔒 Your OTP is 123456").
   Isse inbox me open-rate 15-25% tak badh jati hai.

3. Clean/Clinical Choice:
   Agar clinical/formal look chahiye toh Zero Emojis (SVG icons only) best rehta hai.
   Agar user-friendly/friendly healthcare look chahiye toh uper diye gaye emojis me se
   har template me maximum 1 ya 2 emojis use karna recommended hai.
